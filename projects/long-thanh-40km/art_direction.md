# Art Direction: Long Thanh 40 Km

## Design Read
A calm infrastructure documentary for travelers and residents asking why Long Thanh is far from central Ho Chi Minh City. The film should feel like a measured public-record investigation: access distance is a real downside, capacity figures have careful scopes, and operator claims stay attributed.

## Taste Profile
- Visual variance: 5/10. Each data family gets its own visual grammar, tied together by restrained type and the measuring-line motif.
- Motion intensity: 3/10. Slow drafting reveals, no bouncy easing, no motion that competes with narration.
- Information density: 4/10. One main claim and one supporting qualifier per frame.
- Reference strategy: No AI imagery or archival-looking fabricated imagery. All frames are authored vectors and typography; factual labels cite their sources.

## Palette
- Asphalt ink: `#10141F`, an infrastructure-documentary ground, not a generic corporate gradient.
- Paper: `#E8E6E1`, high-legibility information and map field.
- Runway amber: `#F0A202`, reserved for distance measurements and the single emphasis line.
- Verification teal: `#2E8B87`, used only for cited facts and source tags.
- Muted concrete: `#777E86`, secondary labels and non-data structure.

No gradients, glows, airport glamour renders, or pseudo-official seals.

## Type Personality
Use IBM Plex Sans (or a metrically similar system sans fallback) with tabular numerals. Heavy weight only for a key number; regular for narration-adjacent labels; small but readable source notes. Avoid condensed display type and all-caps shouting.

## Motion Character
Survey-drawing motion: lines extend with a controlled ease-out, blocks assemble in sequence, and data settles without bounce. Typical reveals 12-20 frames at 30 fps. Holds are long enough to read Vietnamese diacritics and units. Transitions connect geometry only when the outgoing and incoming shapes genuinely correspond.

## Layout and Rhythm
Alternate a full-frame regional schematic, a two-track capacity diagram, a phase progression, a proportional land ledger, a clock instrument, and the map reprise. Preserve a stable bottom caption safe area. Keep factual source tags out of the narration caption lane. Every claim's scope must remain visible while the number is shown.

## Signature Device
One amber measuring line: first it compares approximate cited distances, then it returns only in the closing beat as the unresolved access trade-off. It must not become recurring decoration.

## Scene Distinctness
1. Regional schematic map: distance and geography.
2. Abstract terminal cross-section with two independent quantity tracks: phase-one capacity.
3. Three rising phase slabs plus a separately scoped T3 panel: ultimate capacity.
4. Proportional land-use ledger: acreage and governance categories.
5. Instrument clock with city-edge sound contour: ACV's attributed operating rationale.
6. Map reprise with a balanced closing statement: access trade-off and transition teaser.

## Factual Guardrails
- 100 million passengers/year is ultimate whole-airport design capacity after all three phases; 20 million/year is the T3 terminal's design capacity. Do not show a ratio.
- Attribute 24/7, noise, and night-restriction rationale to ACV.
- Do not invent travel duration, road route, noise measurements, airport boundaries, or the historical site-selection rationale.
- Label 01/12/2026 commercial operations as expected/planned, not completed.

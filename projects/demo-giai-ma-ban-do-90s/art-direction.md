# Art Direction — demo-giai-ma-ban-do-90s (v4 Johnny Harris / Vox 3D Tactical Edition)

## Visual Language & Signature Architecture
- **3D Tactical Horizon Camera (`Tactical3DStage`):** Deep `52°–57°` aerial pitch revealing an atmospheric midnight-indigo horizon sky dome with starfield, compass bearing telemetry, and seamless terrain mist blending.
- **Vertical 3D Altitude Beacons (`Pin3DStalk`):** Exact 3D-to-2D projected ground radar ellipses with vertical glowing laser stalks rising `90–180px` off the map surface to hold high-contrast Vox-style tactical HUD flags.
- **3D Extruded Infrastructure:**
  - `Bridge3DExtrusion` in `Scene06Route`: `5,44 km` Tân Vũ – Lạch Huyện sea bridge elevated `38px` above the Nam Triệu mouth on 18 vertical concrete piers with cast water shadows.
  - `Arc3DRoute` in `Scene01Overview` & `Scene03FeederDetour`: 3D parabolic cargo arcs curving high above the East Sea with surface shadow projections.
  - `Extruded3DPolygon` in `Scene09StatusLedger`: Solid 3D teal terminal blocks for operating Berths 1–6 vs dashed holographic 2030 expansion blocks.
- **Scene-Specific Bespoke Set-Pieces (Zero Slide Repetition):**
  - `Scene04PinLabel`: Angled cream archival government dossier (`QĐ 1995/QĐ-CHHĐTVN`) with red verification stamp and yellow marker sweep.
  - `Scene05RiverCrossSectionCard`: 3D isometric urban riverbank cutaway diorama with extruded buildings, shear fractures, and 4-story building trench scale.
  - `Scene07Measure`: Two-lane live ship race (`80 m` narrow turbid river vs `160 m` 2,3-football-pitch superhighway).
  - `Scene08BathymetricProfile`: Side-profile container ship grounding at `-7,0 m` with bow-up tilt & shockwave vs `366 m` (`3,5` football pitches) mega-ship floating safely beside an illuminated 4-story underwater building at `-14,0 m`.
